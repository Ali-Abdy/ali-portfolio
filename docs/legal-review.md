## Impressum: implementation decision

The private/family exception should not simply be assumed for an unrestricted portfolio aimed at prospective employers. § 18(1) MStV covers services beyond exclusively personal or family purposes. **For this release, plan a clearly linked Impressum with confirmed full name and a suitable full postal address unless qualified review establishes an exception.** This is the project's cautious implementation decision, not a court ruling on this particular portfolio. [Current MStV, § 18](https://www.die-medienanstalten.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Rechtsgrundlagen/Gesetze_Staatsvertraege/Medienstaatsvertrag_MStV.pdf)

§ 5 DDG has its own scope for businesslike digital services normally supplied for remuneration. A job-application portfolio is not automatically a registered business, and the site contains no sale of services. Do not invent company, register or VAT details. Reassess if freelance offers, advertising, sales or other business activity are added. [§ 5 DDG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ddg/__5.html)

The media authorities explain the need for readily identifiable, directly accessible, permanently available provider details. A city name, GitHub profile or mailto link alone is not a substitute for the planned notice. If the home address should not be public, obtain advice on a suitable address arrangement; do not invent an address or assume a PO box solves the issue. [Media authorities: Impressum guidance](https://www.die-medienanstalten.de/aufgaben/aufsicht/impressumspflicht/)

No journalist/editor designation is added merely because the site describes software projects. Reassess if an editorial/news publication is introduced. No generic “we accept no liability for all links” clause has been added as a supposed exemption from responsibility.

## Privacy notice: what must be completed

An unrestricted professional portfolio should not rely unquestioningly on the GDPR household exemption. For applicable processing, the notice needs the actual controller, purposes/bases, recipients, retention or meaningful criteria, relevant transfers and visitor rights. Hosting processor arrangements and international transfers require their own checks. The correct treatment depends on the selected services and practices. [GDPR, Articles 2, 6, 13, 28 and Chapter V](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679)

Do not guess that logs are kept for seven or thirty days, that all data stays in Germany/the EU, that a provider contract has been signed, or that every request uses consent as its legal basis. Identify necessary delivery/security processing and ordinary correspondence separately. Document the actual interests/assessment if relying on legitimate interests; use pre-contractual processing only where it fits the communication.

The final DE/EN notice should cover controller/contact, hosting/logs, theme storage, email, outbound links, and applicable rights. Rights must be explained with their conditions, including complaints; do not promise unconditional deletion or portability in all circumstances. The [Saarland data protection authority](https://www.datenschutz.saarland.de/) is a relevant local contact, without limiting a person's right to complain to another competent authority.

A Gmail address is not proof of a particular business processing agreement. Confirm whether the account is consumer Gmail or another arrangement and check the actual terms. No new contact provider or paid service has been selected on the owner's behalf.

## Cookies and browser storage

§ 25 TDDDG concerns storage/access on terminal devices, not just cookies. It contains an exception for storage/access strictly necessary to provide a service explicitly requested by the user. A requested theme preference must be considered under that rule, rather than incorrectly calling all localStorage exempt. [§ 25 TDDDG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ttdsg/__25.html)

The implementation has no identified advertising/analytics consent flow to manage. A cosmetic cookie banner would neither establish consent for a real future tracker nor replace a privacy notice. No banner has been added. Keep the theme limited to the selected display preference; explain it in the completed notice. If optional tracking or embeds are introduced, assess their storage/access first and block consent-dependent operations until a valid choice. Browser permission to store data alone is not consent. [DSK guidance on digital services](https://www.datenschutzkonferenz-online.de/media/oh/OH_Digitale_Dienste.pdf)

If AI generation or material alteration was involved, identify the tool/source and review applicable terms and any disclosure requirements; the source cannot be inferred reliably from appearance. [Usage rights, § 31 UrhG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__31.html); [portraits, § 22 KunstUrhG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kunsturhg/__22.html)
- **Unused decorative image:** no verified provenance found in the repository. It is no longer served by the website, but remains in repository history and the source directory. Confirm permission to keep distributing it through GitHub too. Removal from a page does not erase earlier publication.
- **Font and libraries:** keep the actual copyright/license texts with distribution. The [Geist OFL source](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/geist/OFL.txt) is recorded under `licenses/geist/`. Regenerate notices after updates and review unfamiliar terms rather than treating the script as legal approval.
- **Project claims:** the portfolio describes learning projects and explicit unfinished work. Do not label demonstrations as client work, imply business relationships, publish real customer/user data, or present unfinished applications as production-ready. Recheck screenshots and demo seed data before adding them. Separate repositories are not covered by this review.
- **CV:** before adding a downloadable copy, review third-party contact details, signatures, full home address, birth date and embedded metadata. Include only the information the owner intends to publish; private application material can continue to be sent directly on request.
